## What "AI search optimized" means here

AI assistants don't rank pages — they retrieve *passages* and re-state them. So each page is rewritten to:

- **Lead with the answer.** Every section opens with a 25–50 word direct answer, then supports it. AI pulls the first chunk.
- **Use question headings.** H2s phrased the way people actually type: "How much does divorce mediation cost in Memphis?" not "Fees."
- **Make sentences self-contained.** "Our firm handles..." is useless out of context. "Joni K. Roberts Law and Mediation Office handles..." survives extraction.
- **Anchor the entity.** Full name, city, county, bar admission year, courts of practice, appellate history, and contact details repeated across pages so models build a confident picture of who you are.
- **Add verifiable specifics.** Dates, court names, credential numbers, jurisdictions. AI systems weight concrete, checkable facts far higher than adjectives.
- **Ship structured data.** JSON-LD schema included for each page — this is how machines confirm the facts in your prose.
